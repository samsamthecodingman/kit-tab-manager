Connect your AI assistant Optional

Kit's rules, lists and tidy-ups already work. To let your AI assistant (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor and others) see your tabs, and to use Organise with AI, run this once in a terminal on Linux or macOS:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samsamthecodingman/kit-tab-manager/master/install.sh | sh
  1. Installs Kit's small companion app in your home folder. It needs python3, which most systems already have.
  2. Connects it to your browser and to the AI assistants it finds: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Hermes Agent, Cursor, Windsurf and Claude Desktop.
  3. That's it: this section disappears by itself once Kit is connected.

AI assistants

Connect the assistants you use, so they can see and manage your tabs. Claude Code, Codex and Hermes Agent can also run Organise with AI.

Organise with AI

The AI looks at your tab titles and addresses and sorts them into sensible groups, keeping the groups you already have. It can group, move and rename, but never close a tab or read a page.

Takes 20–60 seconds. You can keep browsing while Kit works.

One-click tidy-ups

Instant, offline and free. Kit walks out to show you what's happening.

Apply my rules

Put each tab in the group your rules say, like recipes into Cooking.

Group by website

Any site with two or more loose tabs gets a group of its own.

Sort each group

Put the tabs inside every group in alphabetical order.

Collapse all groups

Fold every group away for a clean tab bar.

Close duplicates

Close extra copies of the same page. You see the list first.
